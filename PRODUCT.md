# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Investors evaluating early-stage founders (primarily a16z Speedrun reviewers) who spend seconds deciding whether to read further. Also: collaborators, journalists, and creators who arrive from Omar's social media or press coverage. Primary action is credibility formation — the visitor should finish the page convinced Omar is a serious builder worth paying attention to.

## Product Purpose

Omar Zeineddine's personal portfolio, centered on Vlogit but telling the full founder story. Not a resume, not a pure investor pitch. Vlogit is the biggest piece — an iOS social video app in active beta (1,700+ testers, launching end of May 2026) — but the site also captures the personal brand behind it: 200K+ followers built by documenting the journey in public, press coverage (UVA News feature), and the story of who Omar is as a builder. Success means any visitor — investor, creator, or journalist — leaves with a clear mental model of what's being built and why this founder can pull it off.

## Brand Personality

Bold, creative, authentic. The energy of a founder who ships publicly and doesn't wait for permission. Not polished-corporate, not hype-startup. The personality of someone who has been building in front of 200K people for 10 months and has nothing to hide.

## Anti-references

- Generic developer portfolio: project grids, skill badge lists, "About Me" sections, contact forms
- SaaS landing page clichés: hero-metric cards (big number + small label), gradient text, glassmorphism, identical card grids, generic CTA sections
- Corporate or buttoned-up: formal tone, safe choices, no personality
- Overly designed / try-hard: effects and animations that distract from the story
- No referencing other founders' personal sites — should feel like it came from the product and the person, not a template

## Content hierarchy

1. **Vlogit** — the main event. What it is, the traction, the launch timeline.
2. **Building in public** — the personal brand. 200K+ followers, the content, the platforms.
3. **The person** — Omar's story, how he got here, what drives him.
4. **Proof points** — press (UVA News: https://news.virginia.edu/content/hoo-built-app-helping-make-social-media-feel-more-real), social presence, any other achievements worth surfacing.

## Design Principles

1. **Traction over decoration.** Make the numbers feel real through context, not visual treatment. Numbers don't need gradient text — they need a sentence around them.
2. **The full person, not just the pitch.** Investors fund founders, not just products. The site should make Omar feel like a real person with a real story, not a deck in disguise.
3. **Earn the visual confidence.** Bold choices are allowed only when they come from the content — not applied as a style layer on top of it.
4. **Authentic over polished.** Slight roughness is better than over-refinement. This is a builder's site, not an agency showpiece.
5. **Show momentum, not status.** Make the visitor feel the velocity of what's being built, not just a list of credentials.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. High contrast text on dark background (check all body and secondary text). No motion-only information conveyance; animations are additive. Respect prefers-reduced-motion.
